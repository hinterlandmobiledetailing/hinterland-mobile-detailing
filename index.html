The process

We begin with a thorough pre-wash to safely loosen dirt and road grime before hand washing the vehicle. Wheels, tyres and harder-to-reach areas are cleaned separately before the main wash.

The interior is then vacuumed and the main surfaces are cleaned and refreshed before the vehicle receives a final inspection.

Why choose a Maintenance Wash?

Regular professional maintenance prevents dirt and contamination from building up and helps preserve the appearance of your vehicle. It is ideal for vehicles that are already in good condition and need consistent professional care.

Heavy contamination, excessive dirt, mud, sand or pet hair may incur a surcharge.

The process

The Premium Detail starts with a thorough exterior wash and detailed cleaning of the wheels, tyres, wheel arches, trims and other areas where dirt accumulates.

The paintwork is cleaned and lightly decontaminated where required before applying the protective treatment.

Inside, we systematically clean the cabin, including carpets, seats, dashboard, console, door panels and other high-touch areas.

The service is finished with a hydrophobic paint protection treatment, creating a water-repellent layer that helps reduce the adhesion of dirt and makes future maintenance washes easier.

Why choose a Premium Detail?

The Premium Detail is designed for customers who want more than just a clean vehicle. It combines a comprehensive interior and exterior detail with additional paint protection, giving your vehicle a cleaner appearance, enhanced gloss and improved water repellency.

It is ideal for vehicles that need a proper refresh or have not been professionally detailed recently.

The process

The paintwork is thoroughly cleaned and decontaminated to remove embedded contaminants such as iron particles, tar and road grime.

The paint is then carefully inspected and polished where required to improve the finish and create the ideal surface for the coating.

Once the paintwork is fully prepared, the ceramic coating is applied panel by panel and professionally cured to create a durable protective layer.

Why choose a Ceramic Coating?

A ceramic coating provides long-term protection while enhancing the gloss and appearance of your paintwork. Its hydrophobic properties help water, dirt and contaminants bead and release more easily, making the vehicle easier to maintain.

The coating comes with a 5-year warranty, provided that the required maintenance is carried out.

Maintenance required

Ceramic coating is not a "fit and forget" product. Regular maintenance is essential to preserve its performance, hydrophobic properties and warranty.

We recommend regular professional maintenance washes and periodic coating maintenance to keep the coating performing as intended.

The 5-year warranty is subject to the recommended maintenance requirements and proper care of the vehicle.

About me

A local detailer
with a passion for cars.

Hi, I'm Antoine. I'm originally from France and now call Montville home. I started Hinterland Mobile Detailing to bring my experience and passion for car care to the local community here in the Sunshine Coast Hinterland.

Over the past two years, I've worked in vehicle detailing, first looking after mining vehicles and then vehicles used in the rental industry. These experiences taught me how to work with all kinds of vehicles, tackle tough conditions and understand the importance of doing a proper job.

Now, I wanted to take that experience and build something of my own, working directly with local people and providing a more personal service.

Whether it's keeping your everyday car clean, freshening up the family SUV or giving your vehicle some extra care, I enjoy taking the time to get things right. I believe in honest work, attention to detail and treating every car with the same care I'd give my own.

Living in Montville, I'm proud to be part of the local community and to offer a convenient mobile service to people across the Hinterland.

Thanks for supporting a small local business. I look forward to meeting you and looking after your vehicle!
